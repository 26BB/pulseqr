# Go-To-Market Strategy: PulseQR

## Positioning Statement
For independent F&B owners in Pune, PulseQR is an instant feedback tool that intercepts angry customers before they leave bad online reviews. Unlike paper forms or complex CRM software, PulseQR pushes negative alerts directly to your WhatsApp while the customer is still in your cafe, allowing you to save the relationship on the spot.

## Core Channels (Bootstrapped / ₹0 Budget)

### 1. Direct Sales (Walking the street) - Primary Channel
- **Why:** F&B is a localized, relationship-driven business.
- **Action:** Print out beautiful, mock-up QR code standees with the cafe's specific logo on them. Walk into 30 cafes in Koregaon Park/Viman Nagar between 3 PM and 5 PM (low footfall hours). 
- **Pitch:** "I built this for you. Put this on one table for a week for free. If you don't get 10 pieces of feedback, I'll take it back."

### 2. LinkedIn (Building in Public / Founder's Office Signal)
- **Why:** To attract the attention of startup founders and PMs (the meta-goal of this portfolio project).
- **Action:** Document the journey. Share pictures of the MVP, screenshots of Make.com workflows, and photos of handing standees to cafe owners.

### 3. Local F&B WhatsApp/Facebook Groups
- **Why:** High density of target persona.
- **Action:** Share a case study of "How Cafe X intercepted 3 bad Zomato reviews this week using a simple WhatsApp trick." Offer 5 free beta slots.

## Launch Playbook (4-Week Timeline)

### Week -1: Pre-Launch
- Finalize MVP via Tally + Make + WhatsApp.
- Scrape Google Maps for 100 cafes in Pune; find owner names on LinkedIn/Instagram.
- Print 10 physical acrylic QR code standees.

### Week 0: Launch Week (The Ground Hustle)
- **Monday-Wednesday:** Visit 20 cafes in person. Goal: Secure 3 pilot customers.
- **Thursday:** Post the "Building in Public" thread on Twitter & LinkedIn.
- **Friday:** Onboard the 3 pilot customers, place the standees on their tables.

### Week 1: Post-Launch Growth Loops
- **B2B Referral Loop:** Add a small footer to the feedback form: *"Powered by PulseQR. Want this for your business?"* Local cafe owners often visit other local cafes.
- Follow up with pilot customers; secure testimonials.

## Budget
- Software (Tally/Make free tiers): ₹0
- Printing QR Standees (Local print shop): ₹500
- **Total:** ₹500
