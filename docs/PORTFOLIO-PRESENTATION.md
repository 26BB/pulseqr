# Portfolio Presentation Guide (For PM / Founder's Office Interviews)

## How to Structure the Case Study (Notion/Medium)
1. **The Hook:** "How I built a B2B SaaS for Pune cafes in 14 days and helped them intercept X negative reviews."
2. **The Problem:** Explain the local F&B pain point using a real quote from your user interviews.
3. **The Hypothesis:** "I believed owners didn't want software; they wanted alerts on WhatsApp."
4. **The MVP:** Show screenshots of the architecture (Tally -> Make -> WhatsApp). Emphasize speed over perfection.
5. **The Metrics (Results):** Show real numbers. E.g., "Deployed in 3 cafes. 142 scans. 3 critical alerts triggered. 1 churned due to X."
6. **My Learnings:** What failed? (e.g., "Users didn't scan QR codes placed near the register; they only scanned them on the tables.")

## How to Write the LinkedIn Post
> **Headline:** I spent the last 14 days walking into cafes in Pune. I didn't buy coffee; I sold them a SaaS product I built in a weekend. Here's what happened. ☕👇
> 
> **Body:** As someone aiming for a Founder's Office role, I wanted to prove I could go 0 to 1. 
> - **Problem:** Cafe owners hate surprise Zomato reviews.
> - **Solution:** A frictionless QR feedback loop that texts the owner *while* the customer is still sitting there.
> - **Tech Stack:** Completely No-Code (Tally + Make + WhatsApp API).
> - **Results:** 3 active pilots, X feedback submitted, 0 marketing spend.
> 
> **Call to Action:** Looking for an APM / Founder's Office internship in Pune. Check out my full PRD and case study here: [Link]

## Key Talking Points for Interviews
*   **When they ask about "Product Sense":** Talk about how you ruthlessly cut the "Analytics Dashboard" feature because user research showed owners wouldn't log in. You met the user where they are (WhatsApp).
*   **When they ask about "Execution/Hustle":** Highlight the direct sales approach. You didn't hide behind a screen; you walked into cafes and handled rejection.
*   **When they ask about "Data":** Walk them through your AARRR funnel and explain why "Scans per Active Cafe" was your North Star Metric.

## Lessons Learned Template
1. **Product Lesson:** Friction is the enemy. Adding one extra question dropped completion rates by X%.
2. **GTM Lesson:** SME sales in India are pure relationship building. The product matters less than the founder's trust.
3. **Personal Lesson:** Launching is easy; distribution is the actual bottleneck.
