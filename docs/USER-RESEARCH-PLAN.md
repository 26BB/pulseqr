# User Research Plan

## Research Objectives
1. Understand how independent cafe/restaurant owners currently measure customer satisfaction.
2. Validate the assumption that owners fear negative online reviews and would pay to intercept them.
3. Identify the friction points in existing feedback mechanisms (paper forms, Google reviews).

## Target Interview Profile
- **Who:** Founders / Managers of independent cafes or casual dining restaurants in Pune.
- **Location:** Koregaon Park, Viman Nagar, FC Road, Balewadi.
- **Sample Size:** 5-7 qualitative interviews.

## Screener Questions (To ensure fit)
1. Are you the primary decision-maker for the restaurant's operations?
2. Do you have seating for at least 15 people?
3. Are you an independent brand (not a national franchise like McDonald's)?

## Interview Script (15 mins)

### Context Building
1. "Tell me a bit about a typical busy day for you at the cafe."
2. "How much of your time is spent on floor operations versus back-office work?"

### Discovery & Pain Points
3. "How do you currently know if a customer had a good or bad experience today?"
4. "Tell me about the last time you received a negative review on Google or Zomato. How did you feel, and what did you do?"
5. "Have you ever tried collecting feedback in the restaurant (e.g., paper forms, asking directly)? How did that go?"
6. "What is the biggest reason customers *don't* give you feedback to your face?"

### Solution Testing (Hypothesis Validation)
7. "If a customer was unhappy with their food right now, how long would it take for you to find out?"
8. "If you could get an instant WhatsApp message the moment a customer at Table 4 rated their food 2 stars, what would you do with that information?"
9. "How much would saving one negative online review a week be worth to your business in rupees?"

## How to Synthesize Findings (Affinity Mapping)
After the interviews, map quotes into three buckets:
1. **Desirability:** Do they actually care about this problem?
2. **Usability:** Are they too busy to check a dashboard? (Validating the WhatsApp push idea).
3. **Viability:** Will they pay for it, or do they expect it for free?

## Sample Insight Format
> **Hypothesis:** Owners want detailed analytics on customer satisfaction.
> **Finding:** INVALIDATED. 4 out of 5 owners stated they "don't have time to log into software." They only care about actionable alerts.
> **Product Decision:** Scrape the analytics dashboard from the MVP. Build WhatsApp alerts only.
