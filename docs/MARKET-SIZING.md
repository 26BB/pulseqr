# Market Sizing Analysis: F&B Feedback SaaS in India

## Defining the Niche Market
B2B SaaS for micro-to-small independent Food & Beverage (F&B) outlets focusing on customer retention and private feedback loops.

## Total Addressable Market (TAM)
* **Scope:** All independent restaurants and cafes in urban India.
* **Calculation (Top-Down):** There are an estimated 300,000 to 500,000 organized/semi-organized F&B outlets in India. 
* **Value:** Assuming an Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) of ₹500/month (₹6,000/year). 
* **TAM Value:** 500,000 outlets × ₹6,000 = **₹300 Crores / Year (~$36M USD)**.

## Serviceable Available Market (SAM)
* **Scope:** Tech-forward, modern independent cafes, bistros, and casual dining restaurants in Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, Delhi NCR).
* **Calculation:** Tier 1 cities account for roughly 30% of this market. Exclude heavy chain restaurants (who build custom tech). 
* **SAM Value:** 150,000 outlets × ₹6,000 = **₹90 Crores / Year (~$11M USD)**.

## Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) - Year 1 (Pune Focus)
* **Scope:** Independent cafes and casual dining restaurants in Pune (focusing on hubs like KP, Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, Balewadi).
* **Calculation (Bottom-Up):** 
  - There are approximately 2,973 restaurants and 970 cafes in Pune (~4,000 total).
  - ~85% of these are independent operations = 3,400 target outlets.
  - Assuming a highly conservative Year-1 market penetration of 3%.
  - 3,400 × 0.03 = 100 paid customers.
* **SOM Value (Year 1):** 100 outlets × ₹6,000 = **₹6,00,000 ARR**.

## Key Assumptions
1. **Willingness to pay:** Cafe owners will pay ₹499/month, which is the cost of roughly 2-3 cups of premium coffee, making it an easy psychological threshold.
2. **Tech Adoption:** High smartphone penetration means 95%+ of diners can seamlessly scan QR codes.

## Comparable Companies / Market Signals
- **Yotpo / Trustpilot:** E-commerce equivalents, proving the immense value of reviews.
- **Thrive Now / DotPe:** Proves that Indian F&B owners will adopt modern, lightweight tech stacks if the ROI is clear and it bypasses aggregators like Zomato.
